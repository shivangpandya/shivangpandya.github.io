## The Mechanics of Agentic Engineering: Compaction and Linking

I have been following Armin Ronacher for a while now and discovered his series  **The State of Agentic Coding**. 

One of the most profound concepts emerging from the practitioner community right now is how we structure software conventions under intense "agentic pressure". Humans naturally split logic across hundreds of tiny files because our working memory is heavily limited. For an AI agent, however, every file split forces an extra tool call, expanding context windows and risking loop derailment. 

### Compaction & Linking

This brings us to the dual concepts of **Compaction** and **Linking** in agentic workflows:

* **Compaction (Context Management):** A concept I recently learned is Agents rely on aggressive context compaction routines— after a certain set of tokens which causes degradation in performance as more context gets filled up .
