Having spent some time playing around with AI , I am working to find some opinions around how our life will change with AI. I hope to come back and revisit each month , to keep track and add more predictions as I play this game each month

## Predictions 
1. Earlier the constraints were how  engineers had their time valuable for workng on the features and only a certain set of people knew how to code. Hence this subset of people demanded higher salaries . Current constraints with AI include :  How should we build it , which customers should we be talking to , Where should be focusing out time.

2. Fast‑learning juniors can level up quickly; strong seniors can become dramatically more effective

3. Generation of code is not a bottleneck , review of the code is ! 

4. Expecting more software than ever, smaller teams, and faster cycles of shipping software. Anthropic is reaching $39B run-rate with only 2700 employees

5. AI agents can now browse the web, compare prices, and negotiate with service providers completely on their own.Because humans no longer need to manually authorize every digital transaction, the volume of micro-transactions on the web will skyrocket.
