---
title: "OKE (Oracle Kubernetes Engine) is genuinely solid"
publishDate: "2025-01-20"
tags: ["oci", "kubernetes", "containers"]
---

OKE has a fully managed control plane, integrates cleanly with OCI Load Balancers and Block Volumes for PVCs, and supports both virtual and managed nodes. If you're already in the OCI ecosystem, it's worth using over self-managed Kubernetes — you get free control plane nodes and tight IAM integration.
